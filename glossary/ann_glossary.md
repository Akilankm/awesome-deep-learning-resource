# ANN Glossary

**Activation** — nonlinear transformation applied after a pre-activation.

**Backpropagation** — reverse-mode chain-rule procedure computing loss derivatives with respect to parameters.

**Batch** — subset processed together before an optimizer step.

**Calibration** — agreement between predicted confidence and empirical correctness.

**Epoch** — one logical pass through the training dataset.

**Gradient** — vector of partial derivatives of loss with respect to parameters.

**Hidden representation** — intermediate learned coordinates produced by a hidden layer.

**Logit** — unnormalized output score before softmax.

**Loss** — scalar optimization objective.

**Optimizer** — rule converting gradients into parameter updates.

**Train/validation/test** — datasets for parameter learning, development decisions and final evaluation respectively.
