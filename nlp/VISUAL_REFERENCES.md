## Repository policy

- Prefer authoritative or well-documented open-license reference visuals.
- Always keep attribution and license information close to the repository.
- Do not use a diagram just because a concept has a notebook.
- For sparse models, preprocessing, metrics, and data pipelines, executed tables/plots are often clearer than architecture artwork.
- External visuals supplement the explanation; they do not replace the notebook's own reasoning and executed examples.
