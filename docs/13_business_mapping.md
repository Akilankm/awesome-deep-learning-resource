# Business and Production Mapping

ANN concepts map directly to enterprise decisions: features become transaction/document/product/customer signals; hidden layers become learned embeddings; misclassification becomes financial/operational cost; confidence becomes decision risk; drift becomes changing populations or data pipelines.

The final optimization target is not benchmark accuracy alone. It is reliable business value under constraints such as latency, review capacity, risk tolerance, explainability, cost and governance.

## Learning questions
- What assumption does this stage make?
- What can fail silently?
- What evidence would you monitor?
- How does this affect business or production behavior?
